                  Golden rules

                  • Your bank will never ask for your PIN, CVV, OTP or password, by phone, SMS or email.
                  • Knowing your name or account number doesn't prove a caller is real. Scammers buy leaked data.
                  • Anyone asking for gift cards is a scammer.
                  • Can't video call, ever? Don't send them money.
                  • In doubt? Hang up and call the number on the back of your card.
